# Ontaura

**A semantic data platform for connected operations.**

Ontaura is a software platform for managing, connecting and understanding operational data. It provides an established foundation for building applications that work with real-world information, including people, assets, locations, events, documents and time-series data.

At its core, Ontaura uses an ontology-powered knowledge graph to represent entities, their attributes and the relationships between them. This allows information from different sources to be organised within a consistent model, preserving the context needed for operational applications, analytics, automation and AI.

## Who is Ontaura for?

Ontaura is designed for:

* **Organisations** looking for a flexible platform to manage operational information and connect data from multiple systems.
* **Software developers** who want to build applications without developing their own data models, relationship management, security, APIs and automation infrastructure from scratch.
* **Systems integrators and technology providers** who need a configurable foundation for delivering solutions to their customers.

## What problems does it solve?

Operational information is often distributed across databases, applications, devices and documents, making it difficult to understand how different pieces of information relate to one another.

Ontaura provides the underlying capabilities to:

* Organise complex operational information using configurable entity types, attributes and relationships.
* Connect data from multiple sources within a common semantic model.
* Manage assets, locations, people and events alongside historical and real-time measurements.
* Build custom applications, dashboards, reports and automated workflows.
* Provide structured, contextual information to AI agents and other intelligent applications.

Rather than building these foundational capabilities for every new project, developers can use Ontaura to concentrate on solving their specific business requirements.
